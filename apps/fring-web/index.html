Saturn's F ring, rotating frame

Prometheus (schematic) Pandora (schematic)

Frame: turns at the mean motion n0 of the ring core, so the core stays still and the moons drift. Moons are on circular orbits here, not the real ephemeris. Drag to move, scroll to zoom.
