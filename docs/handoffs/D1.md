## Goal and acceptance criteria

## Done
- **A0 exception (2026-11-07):** A3 wrote the new file `crates/fring-model/src/physics/arc.rs` and the lines that register it in `physics/mod.rs` (A2 owns that directory; A0 reviews in the PR). No other change in `fring-model`.
- `physics::arc` (f64 definition, generic over `Real`, wasm32): `ArcGeometry { width, centre, n0 }`; `wrap(pos, vel, t_s) -> Option<Wrapped { pos, vel, k }>` (inertial in, inertial out); `fold` (any number of rotations, for the start of a run); `EDGE_TOL`. The wrap rotates position and velocity together by -/+ the width (at most 2 rotations, else `None`). It conserves |r|, |v|, E, Lz and E - n0 Lz because Saturn's field is axisymmetric; local x, z, vx, vy, vz do not change and y changes by k a0 dtheta.
- GPU: `shaders/arc.wgsl` (entry points `step_d_arc`, `step_dp_arc`, with `_log`/`_replay` variants for chaos, and `t_arc_wrap` for tests), `src/arc.rs` (table layout and the DS Rust twin), `GpuStepper::set_arc`, `GpuBackend` takes `Domain::Arc` (folds the start state into the arc, then `set_arc`). Wrap sites: start of block, before each kick, end of block (every step). Arc data are in the free words 33 and 34 of the kick rows and in the two extra rows; the existing kernels and the bind layout do not change.
- Chaos: the particle kernel logs its rotations in the status buffer (8 words per particle after the 8 + cap words); the shadow replays them, so both take the same k.
- Not done by design: mode C (refused), the moon-radius rule together with the arc (refused).

## Tolerances and why (no existing tolerance was changed)
- **360 deg arc against the full ring (150 steps): 1e-4 km, 1e-8 km/s.** The two kernels do the same operations but the Metal compiler fuses the arithmetic differently, so the f32 rounding of the kick differs (101 of 300 particles bit-identical). Measured 9.2e-6 km and 1.4e-9 km/s, so the limit is about 10 times that, and 1e-4 km is 7e-10 of the radius.
- **Arc against the rotated full ring: 4 times the rounding floor + 1e-6 km (+ 1e-9 km/s).** The floor is measured in the test: the full ring from the start state turned by 0.7 rad, turned back. The wrap adds only the DS error (1e-14 relative, 1e-9 km); the difference is the f32 rounding of the kick, which depends on the rotated numbers.
- **E and Lz drift of the arc run: 2 times the ring drift + 3e-13.** About 3 wraps per particle times 1.3e-14, with margin.
- **Inside the arc at the end: 2e-6 rad** (edge slack 1e-6 plus f32 rounding of the test).
- GPU against twin: the PLAN limit 1e-6 is used; the measured difference is 0.

## The 5 deg, mode D result (5.47e-3 km against a floor of 2.20e-3 km, factor 2.5)
It does not come from the number of rotations. Split by |m| (the number of widths the particle moved), the position difference is 5.2e-3, 5.5e-3, 4.6e-3 and 4.8e-3 km for |m| = 0, 1, 2, 3, and the particles with |m| = 0 were never wrapped. In mode D+ (J2 term in DS) the same runs give 4.5e-5 km against a floor of 4.0e-5 (factor 1.1). So the cause is the larger rounding noise of mode D (f32 J2 term, about 100 times mode D+), where two different rounding realisations differ by this amount; the floor is the maximum of one more realisation and the maximum of 600 particles with a heavy tail, so a factor 2 to 3 between two maxima is expected. I did not prove this with a third realisation; the |m| split and the D+ result are the evidence. The tolerance (4 times the floor) was not changed.

## Findings for others
1. **Cells (A4 K2 `Domain`, A3 K5-BIN `BinGeometry`):** after the wrap the rotating-frame longitude is in [phi0 - eps, phi0 + dtheta + eps], eps = 1e-6 rad (`EDGE_TOL`, 0.14 km at the core). Clamp the cell index to 0 and n_y - 1 after rounding. No mod is needed.
2. **Initial longitudes (A0 puts this in D1-CPU):** the kernel makes at most 2 rotations per site. `GpuBackend` therefore folds the start state into the arc (`ArcGeometry::fold`). Folding a ring distribution is not uniform in the arc when 360/dtheta is not a whole number. The generator must draw longitudes inside the arc.
3. **Bug found and fixed here:** without an edge slack, one particle in 10^5 over 1500 steps was rotated to the other edge and back by the f32 rounding of the test and then dropped. `EDGE_TOL` fixes it (found by `arc_cost`).
4. **D1-CPU (A2, A5):** call `ArcGeometry::wrap(pos, vel, t_s)` after each step in `fring-cpu`; `fring-run` can accept `domain.kind = "arc"` (`check_supported` still refuses it). The GPU wraps at three kinds of site per block; CPU and GPU agree in statistics, not in bits.
5. With moons, the energy jumps at a wrap by the change of the moon potential. This is the PLAN 7.6 approximation measured by D2. `GpuStepper` (web) has no domain field: call `set_arc` and fold the start state yourself.
