# W2: live test-particle mode
Role: A7  Branch: ws/a7-w2  Status: review
Updated: 2026-11-02 18:35

## Goal and acceptance criteria
Live test-particle mode using the `fring-gpu` shaders: about 10^5 particles, controls for time speed, Pandora on/off, and initial band. Done when: it runs at 30 fps or more on the Mac mini (Chrome and Safari 26); it deploys as a static site (a static `apps/fring-web/dist/` that any static file server can serve; no public host in W2).

## Done
- `src/live.rs` (host-testable): `Setup` (params, 10-year ephemeris, `System` with or without Pandora, initial bands), `StepPlanner`, `Governor`, inertial `view_transform`, `ProjectionUniform` and `project_twin`, `head_instances`.
- `shaders/project.wgsl`: compute pass from the double-single state buffer (ICR-005 layout) to the instance buffer (`VERTEX | STORAGE`), relative to the eye.
- `src/web.rs`: state machine (checking, preparing, running, failed). `SelfTest::submit` in `create`, `poll` once per frame; if it fails, the page shows the message and no stepper is made. `WhKernels::compile`, `GpuStepper` (Mode Dp, step 360 s, block 128), projection pass, render pass. Moon dots are drawn at `stepper.time_s()`.
- Failure flag: once per second `status_buffer()` is copied to a MAP_READ buffer and mapped; later frames read it. A failed particle stops stepping and shows "a particle failed (index i); reset to continue".
- Step planner: fractional steps are carried; cap 200 steps per frame, lowered by the governor when the frame rate drops below 28 fps; no stepping when 2 frames are still in flight (`on_submitted_work_done`).
- Controls: Pandora checkbox (removes only Pandora's track from the particles' system; Prometheus keeps its real path; Pandora's dot hidden), initial band (Core, Band a0 +- a0 e, Between the moons, Custom with two km inputs), speed 1 to 500 h/s, Restart, 4 views, fps counter, `?bench`.
- Readout: simulated time and the end of the ephemeris span (10 years minus 2 days); the run restarts from t = 0 near the end with a note.
- The W1 analytic `scene.rs` is deleted (no test used it as a reference); ring params come from `fring_model::params::ring_from_toml_str`; `serde` and `toml` are no longer dependencies of fring-web. `Cargo.lock` changed (dependencies of fring-web).
- Native GPU tests (ignored, need a GPU): `tests/project_gpu.rs` (M16: `project.wgsl` against the Rust twin and f64; 3 views, eye 3 km and 2000 km away, 2000 particles each, lo parts non-zero) and `tests/live_gpu.rs` (Setup + GpuStepper, Pandora on and off, 3 bands, 300 steps, no failure, radii in range).
- M16 finding: the first projection shader multiplied both differences by a runtime 1.0 (the `ds.wgsl` guard). On the M4 it lost the low parts (error 2.3e-3 km at 3.6 km from the eye; the compiler pulled the common factor out and re-associated). The final shader passes the high difference through `select(x, 0.0, one < 0.5)`. The worst error is now 0.2 of the tolerance (1e-6 km + 6e-7 d, which is 1.3e-3 px at 100 px/km for d = 20 km).

## Arithmetic
Dp at 9.5e8 particle-steps/s for 10^5 particles: one step of all particles 0.105 ms. At 500 h/s: 5000 steps/s, 83 steps per frame at 60 fps (167 at 30 fps), 53 % of the GPU. Default 6 h/s: 1 step per frame at 60 fps. Below about 6 h/s at 60 Hz the motion is stepwise (one 360 s step every few frames); the slowest speed is 1 h/s (one step every 6 frames at 60 Hz).

Browser steps (not done by A7):
1. Page opens; the status line shows "Checking GPU arithmetic...", then "Preparing the moons (a few seconds)...", then clears. No error box. Footer shows "GPU: <name>".
2. Inertial view: about 10^5 dots in a thin ring, two moon dots (orange Prometheus, green Pandora), everything anticlockwise.
3. The readout shows "t = ... of 3650.0 d", the view width, fps (at least 30) and steps per frame (1 at the default speed).
4. Speed slider to the maximum (21 d/s): fps stays at least 30, or "(speed limited)" appears. `?bench` prints mean fps, 5th-percentile fps and the worst frame after 13 s; pass: mean at least 30 and 5th percentile at least 25.
5. Untick Pandora: the green dot disappears, t = 0. Tick again: it returns, t = 0.
6. Particles select: Core, Band, Between the moons: t resets; the km inputs show the range. Edit a km value: "Custom band", t resets. Min above max gives a red-orange message and changes nothing.
7. Views: Rotating (core still), On Prometheus, On Pandora (the moon at the centre, Saturn straight down). Zoom to a few km at the ring in each view: no jitter.
8. Pause, Restart, Reset view work. Console: no errors.
9. If the self-test fails the page shows "The GPU arithmetic check failed" and no motion.
10. Failure flag: a particle that becomes unbound shows "a particle failed (index i); reset to continue" about a second later, and stepping stops until Restart (hard to trigger by hand).
