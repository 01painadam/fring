# C4: test particles with moons, rayon, initial conditions
Role: A2  Branch: ws/a2-c4  Status: done
Updated: 2026-11-01

## Goal and acceptance criteria
The CPU backend agrees with REBOUND for 100 particles over 100 orbits (V3): position difference < 1 km for non-chaotic orbits (PLAN section 10). Also: moons in the kick (ADR 0004), rayon over particles with a result that does not depend on the thread count, initial conditions from the config distributions.

## Done
- `fring-model/src/physics/moon_kick.rs`: `indirect_acceleration`, `moon_direct_acceleration`, `kick_acceleration`, `KickTable` (moon positions and indirect term per kick, kick at the middle of each step), `wh_steps_moons`.
- `fring-model/src/physics/initial.rs`: `epicycle_state`, `initial_particle`, `generate_particles` (Philox `INITIAL_CONDITIONS`, counter index = id). Core, band, clump; z = 0 except the clump's z offset. Config doc comments say that `eccentricity` is the epicyclic amplitude and that a clump has the circular velocity of its centre.
- `fring-cpu`: `CpuBackend` accepts an ephemeris (must cover the run), AoS state, blocks of 4096 steps, `par_chunks_mut(128)` with rayon. `integrate::advance_chunk` is the only particle loop. Energy is `None` with moons.
- Tests: `crates/fring-cpu/tests/v3.rs`, `threads.rs`; unit tests in `moon_kick.rs` and `initial.rs`.
- ADR 0005 (test definition).

## Results
- V3 max position difference: 150 steps/orbit 5.810 km; 600: 0.3633 km; 1500: 0.0579 km (pure h²).
- Moons against `v3_moons.csv`: 3.07e-4 km. Encounter metric min b/(v_rel h): 19.9 (150), 79.6 (600).
- Threads: 1, 3, 8 and default pool give identical bits, equal to a serial loop.
- Release throughput (Apple M4): 9.7e6 particle-steps/s on 1 thread, 6.7e7 on 10 threads (10⁴ particles, 1460 steps, two moons).

## Open questions / blockers
- No sub-stepping near a moon. The V3 particles stay 459 km or more from a moon (about 7.3 Hill radii). Particles that cross a moon's orbit need per-particle sub-stepping (PLAN 7.3) in a later task.
- `Backend::new` clones the ephemeris (76 MB for 30 years).
