## Decisions made (link ADRs)
- `docs/adr/0004-saturn-centred-equations.md` (model).
- One orbit is T0 = 2 pi / n(a0), n from J2+J4+J6 (53445.914 s), not the Kepler period.
- Initial states are Cartesian first-order epicycles; e and i are epicyclic amplitudes, not osculating elements.
- Non-chaotic selection by an along-velocity shadow (growth factor at most 10) and 5 Hill radii from the moons. A quiet orbit has a growth factor of about 3 (an epicycle of the offset size), so the factor measures sensitivity, not chaos.
