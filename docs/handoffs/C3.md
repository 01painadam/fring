# C3: moon ephemeris integrator and Hermite interpolation
Role: A2  Branch: ws/a2-c3  Status: review
Updated: 2026-11-01

## Goal and acceptance criteria
Interpolation error at mid-interval < 1e-3 km; moon orbits agree with A6's REBOUND run (V2): position difference below 1 km up to the first epoch where the runs differ by 1 km (report it), statistics after it.

## Done
- ICR-002 (Accepted): `MoonTrack.accelerations_km_s2` (required), checked by `MoonEphemeris::validate`. Default interval 1800 s, quintic Hermite.
- `fring-model/src/physics/`: `moon_dynamics.rs` (`MoonSystem`, ADR 0004), `ias15.rs` (IAS15 from Rein and Spiegel 2015, compensated sums, dense output), `ephemeris.rs` (`build_ephemeris`, `build_ephemeris_with`, `interpolate_moons`, `interpolate_moons_into`, `MoonInit`), `hermite.rs` (`hermite5`, `hermite3`).
- `fring-model/src/params.rs`: `saturn_from_toml_str`, `moons_from_toml_str`.
- Tests: `crates/fring-model/tests/moons.rs`, `moons_v2.rs`.

## Results
- Mid-interval error, 60 days, quintic / cubic: 300 s 6.7e-9 / 5.9e-4 km; 600 s 4.2e-7 / 9.5e-3 km; 1800 s 3.1e-4 / 0.77 km.
- V2, 20 years, 6 h rows: max |dr| 4.5e-4 km (Prometheus), 5.9e-4 km (Pandora); the 1 km epoch is not reached. Mean motions agree to 4e-10 relative (587.28445, 572.79041 deg/day), synodic period 24.8374 d on both, anti-alignment 6.2374 yr on both.
- Conservation (1e-9): one moon with J, 1 yr, |dE/E| 4.6e-15, |dLz/Lz| 2.2e-15; two moons without J, half a year, |dE/E| 1.7e-15, |dL|/|L| 9.4e-16.
- Epsilon: the difference between 1e-9, 1e-10 and 1e-11 is at the round-off floor, 3e-6 km after 1 yr and 1e-4 to 2e-4 km after 20 yr, and does not shrink with epsilon.
- Times (release): 20-yr table 0.6 s; 30-yr table 1.0 s (75.7 MB f64 for two moons); fast tests 3 s and 1 s in a debug build.

## Open questions / blockers

- The reference ends at 20 years; a 30-year table has no V2 check beyond 20 years.
