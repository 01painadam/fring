# ICR-011: Wisdom-Holman drift about an effective GM (μ_eff at a0)
Requested by: A0 (from E1-DIAG)  Date: 2026-11-08
Status: accepted (AJ, 2026-11-09). AJ chose this direction on 2026-11-08 ("ICR for μ_eff drift") and accepted this text as written on 2026-11-09.

## What

The Wisdom-Holman (WH) split changes. The Kepler drift is about an effective GM, μ_eff, and not about GM. The kick takes the difference. The total force does not change.

1. **μ_eff.** μ_eff = n(a0)² a0³, with n(a0) the exact in-plane circular rate of the J2, J4, J6 field (`physics::frequencies`):
   μ_eff = GM (1 + 3/2 J2 q² − 15/8 J4 q⁴ + 35/16 J6 q⁶), q = R / a0.
   a0 is `[frame] reference_semi_major_axis_km` of the run config (already required; no config change). At a0 = 140,220 km: μ_eff / GM − 1 = 4.5848 × 10⁻³ (μ_eff = 38,105,113.13 km³ s⁻²).
2. **Drift.** Every WH drift uses μ_eff: `wh_step`, `wh_steps`, `wh_steps_moons`, the sub-stepping drift in `fring-cpu/src/extended.rs` (line 221) and the MEGNO shadow (it uses the same steps).
3. **Kick.** The kick acceleration is the old one plus (μ_eff − GM) r / |r|³:
   a_kick = a_J(r) + (μ_eff − GM) r / |r|³ + a_moons(r) + a_indirect.
   On the circular orbit at a0, the radial part of a_J and the new term cancel, so the drift alone follows that orbit exactly.
4. **API (proposal; A2 may choose other names, A0 reviews).** `SaturnField<T>` gets a field `drift_gm_km3_s2`. `SaturnField::from_params` sets it to GM, so code that does not ask for the split keeps the old behaviour bit for bit. A new method `with_drift_radius(self, a0_km)` sets it to μ_eff. A new method gives the kick part of the Saturn field: `acceleration_without_point_mass(r) + (drift_gm − gm) r / |r|³`. The CPU and GPU backends build the field with `with_drift_radius(a0)`.
5. **GPU.** `crates/fring-gpu/shaders/wh.wgsl`: the drift uses μ_eff and 1/μ_eff in place of `prm_gm` and `prm_inv_gm`, in the double-single path (`kepler_c`) and the fast path. Both kick functions add the new term. The Rust twins in `crates/fring-gpu/src/wh.rs` change in the same way.
6. **Not changed.** The total force (`SaturnField::acceleration`, the moon and indirect terms), the leapfrog `dkd_step`, IAS15, the `Backend` trait, the config schema and the snapshot format.

## Why

E1-RUN layer 2 fails one limit at the production step h = 356.25 s: 29 particles of 300 come inside 1 R_H of Prometheus, against 24 in IAS15 (limit ±4.9). E1-DIAG (`docs/handoffs/E1-DIAG.md`, branch `ws/a5-e1-diag`) found that the error scales as h² and starts at passes of 1.2 to 3 R_H, after an along-track phase offset of 2 to 5 km builds up before the pass. The Rust WH at h is 13 to 35 times worse than REBOUND WHFast at the same h (median |a − a_IAS15| for passes under 500 km: WHFast 0.31 km, Rust 3.9 km, Rust at h/4 0.37 km). E1-DIAG did not find the cause.

**Cause (A0, 2026-11-08).** The drift is a Kepler orbit about GM. A particle on a circular orbit of the J2 field is faster than a Kepler circle by a factor (1 + ε)^½, ε = 4.58 × 10⁻³ at 140,220 km. So in the drift it is on a Kepler ellipse with e of about ε, and the kick bends it back once per step. The split error of WH is of order ε² (n h)², and it is secular in the mean longitude. A0 measured it with the shared Rust `wh_steps` (no moons, f64, start on the exact circular orbit, one Julian year of 88,582 steps, error against n(r) t from `physics::frequencies`; a small program outside the repository that calls `wh_steps` and, for μ_eff, `kepler_drift` and `acceleration_without_point_mass`; test T1 below repeats it in the repository):

| r (km) | drift about GM, h | GM, h/4 | GM, h/16 | drift about μ_eff, h | μ_eff, h/4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 139,380 (Prometheus) | −13.50 km | −0.844 km | −0.053 km | −0.084 km | −0.006 km |
| 139,500 (band edge) | −13.48 km | −0.843 km | −0.054 km | −0.072 km | −0.005 km |
| 140,220 (a0) | −12.79 km | −0.799 km | −0.050 km | +0.0001 km | −0.001 km |
| 141,500 (band edge) | −11.94 km | −0.746 km | −0.047 km | +0.109 km | +0.007 km |

The ratio h to h/4 is 16.0, so the error is h². With eccentric and inclined starts (error at h against h/16 of the same method, one year): e = 0.003, i = 0.05° at 140,220 km: GM −11.22 km, μ_eff +0.009 km; at 139,500 km: −11.89 and −0.050 km; at 141,500 km: −10.43 and +0.098 km; e = 0.01, i = 0.2° at 140,220 km: −7.38 and +0.149 km. So μ_eff makes the error 70 to 100,000 times smaller over the band of the chaos map (139,500 to 141,500 km). The cost per step is the same (one more term in the kick).

**Why WHFast is better at the same h.** The E1-REF stand-in integrates the moons with WHFast too (`validation/scripts/e1_standin.py`, line 8), with the same J force as a kick. So the moons have nearly the same phase error as the particles (the model gives −13.1 km per year for Prometheus and −12.4 for a ring particle), and the relative drift is about 0.7 km per year: 17 times less. The Rust moons are exact (tabulated), so the particles slip by the full 12 km per year relative to Prometheus. E1-DIAG measured lam(h) − lam(h/4) = −2 to −5 km at day 100 to 180; the model gives −11.5 km per year × (100 to 180) / 365 = −3.2 to −5.7 km.

**How much it matters for science.** The slip is a mean-motion error of 2.3 × 10⁻⁸ n (12 km / (140,220 km × 3,724 rad per year)). For the synodic rate with a moon this equals an error in a of (2/3)(2.3 × 10⁻⁸)(140,220 km) = 2.2 m. In a large ensemble that is uniform in longitude, it acts like a small shift of the start phases. So the bias of ensemble statistics is probably small. It is large for a test that follows the same particles through their passes, as E1 does: a timing shift of about 27 s changes the epicyclic phase at the pass, so b changes by about 1 km and the kick of a pass at 80 km changes by about 2.5 %. The E1-RUN realisation study used a start offset of 1 × 10⁻⁶ km, so it did not measure the spread of independent samples. A0 does not claim that the layer 2 count of 29 is a bias rather than sampling noise. The change is still right: it removes a known integrator error at no cost, it makes the E1 comparison like for like, and science runs do not need h/4 (4 times the cost).
