# ICR-007: eccentric band distribution (`kind = "eccentric_band"`)
Requested by: A8 (A2-ANALYSIS plan), written by A0  Date: 2026-11-02
Status: Accepted by A0 2026-11-02. AJ chose this option (in place of 22 single-a `core` runs) in the Claude Code session.

## What

A new variant of the config enum `Distribution` (`[particles.distribution]`, `docs/PLAN.md` section 7.4):

```toml
[particles.distribution]
kind = "eccentric_band"
min_semi_major_axis_km = 140150.0
max_semi_major_axis_km = 140700.0
eccentricity = 0.0024          # epicyclic amplitude, as for `core`; in [0, 1)
apse_longitude_deg = 353.78    # longitude of pericentre
```

- `crates/fring-model/src/config.rs`: `Distribution::EccentricBand { min_semi_major_axis_km, max_semi_major_axis_km, eccentricity, apse_longitude_deg }`. Validation: the band edges as for `band` (both > 0, min < max), eccentricity finite and in [0, 1), apse finite.
- `crates/fring-model/src/physics/initial.rs`: the guiding semi-major axis is uniform in [min, max] and the mean longitude λ is uniform in [0, 2π). They use the same Philox draws as `band` (counter word 0 of particle `id`). The state is the first-order epicycle of `core`, with e and mean anomaly M = λ − ϖ.

`band`, `core` and `clump` do not change.

## Why

A2-ANALYSIS (b) compares the chaos of core-like eccentric orbits with circular orbits across the quiet zone, bin by bin (25 km bins). Before this change, the only way to get an eccentric orbit with the apse aligned was `core` with sigma = 0: one exact a for each bin and run. Near the core, the first-order resonances of Prometheus are about 13 km apart: m = 2 a_P / (3 Δa) = 2 × 139,380 / (3 × 1,120) ≈ 83, so Δa / m ≈ 13.5 km. Resonance overlap reaches only to about 139,720 km (1.3 μ^(2/7) a_P ≈ 340 km). So one a per bin can sit on a resonance or between two, and that biases the comparison with the circular map. That map spreads a uniformly over each bin.

The circular control is the same config with `eccentricity = 0` (or `band`) and the same seed. Then the eccentric and circular runs have the same a and λ for each particle id, and the same bins and counts.

## Migration

None. The change is additive. Old configs and old snapshots (`meta.json` config) parse and give the same states, bit for bit. With `eccentricity = 0`, `eccentric_band` gives the states of `band` with the same seed, equal as numbers (a zero velocity component can have the other sign). Tests:

- `physics::initial::tests::eccentric_band_shares_the_band_draws_and_has_the_apse` (5,000 particles): e = 0 states equal the `band` states; for e = 0.0024, (|r| − a) / (a e) = −cos M to 1e-9 for each particle, and the radial speed has the sign of sin M.
- `tests/config.rs::eccentric_band_parses_round_trips_and_is_checked`: parse, TOML round trip, and errors for e = 1, e = −0.1, a NaN apse and min = max.
