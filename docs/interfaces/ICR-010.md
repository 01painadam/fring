# ICR-010: GPU bounds of K1-T6 (energy) and K1-T8 for the stochastic model (K5)
Requested by: A4 (task K5)  Date: 2026-11-08
Status: Accepted by A0 2026-11-08, with one change: the T8a bound is 10⁻¹¹ km/s, not 10⁻⁸ km/s (AJ decision 2026-11-08, see "A0 decision"). `SPEC.md` section 11 has the GPU bounds.

## What

`SPEC.md` section 11 gives the K1-T6 energy bounds (10⁻¹² for ε = 1, 10⁻¹⁰ for ε = 0.5) and the K1-T8 bounds (10⁻¹³ km/s for (a), 10⁻¹² km/s for (b)) in f64. The GPU changes velocities with f32 values of Δu and builds u from the double-single state, so these f64 values cannot hold. This ICR proposes the GPU bounds. The f64 bounds of K2 and K3 do not change. No other tolerance changes: K1-T1 (10⁻⁶ relative, SPEC 4 and 11), T4 (2 %), T5 (2 %), T7 (3 %), the momentum rule of T6 (SPEC 11: ε_mach replaced by the double-single unit round-off), and the CPU against GPU rule (4 combined SE) are as in SPEC.

| Test | f64 bound (SPEC) | GPU bound (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| T6 energy, ε = 1: \|K_end − K₀\| / K₀ | 10⁻¹² | 8 u_f32 N_coll ⟨g²⟩ / K₀, u_f32 = 2⁻²⁴, ⟨g²⟩ = 8 c₀² |
| T6 energy, ε = 0.5: \|K₀ − K_end − Σ ΔK\| / K₀ | 10⁻¹⁰ | the same bound |
| T8a: \|u\| of a circular orbit, and \|u_GPU − u_f64\| | 10⁻¹³ km/s | 10⁻¹¹ km/s (proposed 10⁻⁸; A0 decision) |
| T8b: dispersion of the peculiar velocity with collisions against without, after 10 orbits | 10⁻¹² km/s | 10⁻⁸ km/s |

## Derivation

**T6 energy.** Δu is an f32 vector made from g in f32. A relative error of 2⁻²⁴ in each component of Δu changes the pair energy by the dot product of the true relative velocity g and the error of Δu. This is at most 2 u_f32 |g| |Δu|, and |Δu| ≤ |g|. The rounding of g itself (u − ū in f32, SPEC 12) gives a similar term. So each collision adds at most about 4 u_f32 |g|² in absolute value, and the same for the loss ΔK of the tally (a relative rounding u_f32 of ΔK, which is smaller). Without any cancellation, the sum over N_coll collisions is 4 u_f32 N_coll ⟨g²⟩ with ⟨g²⟩ = 8 c₀² for a Maxwellian (Appendix A, ⟨g³⟩/⟨g⟩ = 8c²). The bound doubles this (a safety factor 2) and divides by K₀ = (3/2) N c₀². The bound is a straight sum, but the real error is a random walk of single errors, so the measured value is far below it (see below). The change of v in double-single (6 × 10⁻¹⁵ relative) adds nothing that matters.

**T8a.** SPEC 12 asks for an error of u of at most 10⁻³ c, and gives c = 1 cm/s, so 10⁻⁸ km/s. This bound is that value for the DS computation of u = v − v_circ φ̂.

**T8b.** The same 10⁻³ c for the change of the dispersion. The stepper itself leaves a circular orbit with |u| about 1.6 × 10⁻⁶ km/s after 10 orbits (A3's Wisdom-Holman step in DS; not an error of the collision code). So the test compares two GPU runs, with and without collisions, and not with zero. It cannot compare the states (a small velocity change moves the phase after 10 orbits: the largest state difference is 3.9 × 10⁻⁵ km/s).

## Measured (M4, Metal, release, 2026-11-08)

- T6 energy, no bulk: ε = 0.5: 3.3 × 10⁻⁸ (bound 1.3 × 10⁻⁵); ε = 1: 3.0 × 10⁻⁷ (bound 2.6 × 10⁻⁵). With the bulk velocity (16.5, −3, 1) km/s: 3.4 × 10⁻⁸ and 3.2 × 10⁻⁷. Momentum: 1.5 × 10⁻¹⁸ km/s (bound 8.5 × 10⁻¹⁵) without bulk; 6.2 × 10⁻¹³ km/s (bound 3.1 × 10⁻⁹) with bulk.
- T8a: largest \|u\| of 10⁵ circular orbits 5.7 × 10⁻¹³ km/s; largest difference to the f64 function 5.2 × 10⁻¹³ km/s (random u up to 0.05 km/s).
- T8b: difference of the rms \|u\| 9.9 × 10⁻¹¹ km/s (20,000 particles, 1,500 steps).

## Who is affected

A4 (K5 tests), A0 (SPEC 11 text after AJ decides), A3 (the stepper noise of circular orbits is a fact, not a request). No code interface changes.

## Migration

If A0 accepts: A0 adds a paragraph to `SPEC.md` section 11 (GPU bounds) with the table above. If A0 rejects a bound: the test in `crates/fring-gpu/tests/gpu_collide.rs` with that bound is changed to the value A0 gives.

## A0 decision

2026-11-08: **Accepted, with T8a = 10⁻¹¹ km/s.** AJ chose this in the Claude Code session ("Accept, T8a = 1e-11").

- T6 energy and T8b: accepted as proposed. The energy margins are 80 to 400 (measured 3.0 × 10⁻⁷ to 3.4 × 10⁻⁸ against bounds 2.6 × 10⁻⁵ and 1.3 × 10⁻⁵). T8b: 9.9 × 10⁻¹¹ against 10⁻⁸, a margin of 100.
- T8a: 10⁻⁸ km/s is the physics need (10⁻³ c), but the DS computation of u is 2 × 10⁴ times better than that (5.7 × 10⁻¹³ km/s). With 10⁻⁸ the test would not see a change that makes u 1,000 times worse. 10⁻¹¹ km/s keeps a margin of 17.5 (circular orbits, 5.7 × 10⁻¹³) and 19 (random u, 5.2 × 10⁻¹³) and still catches a DS-to-f32 mistake: one f32 rounding of v at 16.5 km/s is 16.5 × 2⁻²⁴ = 9.8 × 10⁻⁷ km/s, which is 10⁵ times the bound.
- Both T8a asserts in `peculiar_velocity_equals_the_f64_function` (`|u_GPU − u_f64|` and the circular `|u|`) use 10⁻¹¹ km/s. A0 made the change on `ws/a4-k5` and added the GPU bounds to `SPEC.md` section 11.
