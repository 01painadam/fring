3. **J6.** PLAN section 2 excludes J6. With J2 + J4 only, ϖ̇ at the core is 0.0021 °/d lower than with J6 (23° over 30 years), and the resonance moves by 0.056 km. That is the size of the resonance uncertainty, and the core is under 1 km wide. Adding J6 is one more term in the force (task C1) and one more field in `SaturnParams` (an ICR). **A0 recommends adding J6.** J8 to J12 together change ϖ̇ by only 0.0001 °/d.

A0 recommends that this goes into the first chaos-map analysis (A1-ANALYSIS) as a measured quantity. It should not block phase 1.
5. **Observations.** Confirm or change the priorities A/B/C in section 6 of `docs/literature/summary.md` (PLAN 13, question 4). Priority A now: core a, e, apse rate, width, persistence, the 110-lobe arcs, and the chaos of the moons.
