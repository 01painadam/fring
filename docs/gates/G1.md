## Moon chaos (V2, REBOUND; Rust C3 agrees with it)

Model: Saturn J2 to J6, Prometheus, Pandora; no other moons (PLAN section 2).

| Quantity | V2 | Literature (`docs/literature/summary.md`) |
|---|---|---|

| Mean-motion wander, 20 yr peak-to-peak | 2.3 °/yr (Prometheus), 2.6 °/yr (Pandora) | 1.8 and 3.1 °/yr |
| Lyapunov rate | 0.14 to 0.49 /yr (finite-time, along-velocity shadows) | 0.3 to 0.35 /yr |
| 121:118 resonant angles | All four alternate between circulation and short libration | Four overlapping components |

- **Interpretation (A0, corrected):** the two-moon model reproduces the timescales well, and its chaos agrees with the literature.
  - Between years 1 and 12, an offset along the velocity grows by a factor of 11 (1 m offset) to 25 (1 mm offset). That is a rate of 0.22 to 0.29 per year, or a Lyapunov time of 3 to 5 years (literature: 0.3 to 0.35 per year).
  - The 1 km horizon is not reached in 20 years only because the start offset is small: 1 m × e^(0.3 × 20) ≈ 0.4 km.
  - The growth flattens after about 12 years, so these are finite-time rates, not a converged exponent.
  - Offsets with a radial part grow faster, but that is mostly Kepler shear.
  - An earlier draft of this file called the chaos "mild". That was wrong.
- Plots: `validation/reports/v2_chaos.png`, `v2_resonance.png`, `v2_closest_approach.png`.

## Decisions made in phase 1 (for AJ to accept or reverse)

1. **ADR 0003: Wisdom-Holman replaces the leapfrog as the baseline.**
   - At 150 steps per orbit, the leapfrog slows the apse precession at the core by 9.7 %.
   - After 100 orbits its along-track error is about 52,000 km.
   - Wisdom-Holman gets ϖ̇ to 0.001 %.
   - This is the largest change to the plan.
2. **ADR 0004: indirect terms.** The equations include the acceleration of Saturn by the moons, and the (GM + GMᵢ) two-body term for moons.
3. **ADR 0002 and ADR 0005: test definitions.**
   - C1 energy: the literal 10⁻¹⁰ limit is applied on the leapfrog's own circular orbit.
   - C4: the strict REBOUND test runs at 600 steps per orbit. The production step has a measured bound.
   - No tolerance was relaxed.
4. **ICR-002:** `MoonTrack` stores accelerations for quintic Hermite interpolation. This keeps the 30-year table at 76 MB instead of 303 MB.
5. **Initial conditions:** the config `eccentricity` is the epicyclic amplitude, not an osculating element. A clump moves with its centre's circular velocity.
