### Speed against PLAN section 11 (test particles, no collisions)

The production step is 150 steps per orbit (gate G1). A 30-year run is then 2.7 × 10⁶ steps. The 8 h budget needs 9.4 × 10⁷ particle-steps/s for 10⁶ particles and 9.4 × 10⁸ for 10⁷.

| N | Target | GPU D+, chaos off | GPU D+, chaos on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10⁶ | must | 47 min (10 times the needed rate) | 1.6 h (5.0 times) |
| 10⁷ | stretch | **8.1 h (0.98 times; budget 8 h)** | 16.4 h (0.49 times) |

- These numbers are for test particles without collisions. The collision kernels (phase 4, A4) will use part of the margin. B1 is rerun then.
- At the PLAN's original 100 steps per orbit, 10⁷ particles would take 5.4 h.

## First science result (A1-ANALYSIS)

Full note: `docs/results/a1-analysis.md` (A8, with A0 review corrections). Test particles in a fixed field of two moons, no collisions, GPU D+ for the maps and CPU f64 for the rest.

### 1. Chaos map: no quiet band, but a quiet zone

Circular orbits, uniform in radius and in longitude, 2 × 10⁵ particles (5 and 10 years) and 1.1 × 10⁵ particles (30 years). Chaos indicator as in ADR 0008. λ = 2 × median ⟨Y⟩ / t.

| Radius window (km) | Chaotic at 10 yr | Chaotic at 30 yr | λ (per year) at 10 / 30 yr |
|---|---|---|---|
| 139,700 to 139,950 (inner edge) | 98 % | not run | 8.4 / not run |
| 139,950 to 140,150 (inner flank) | 73 % | 91 % | 2.0 / 2.2 |
| **140,197 to 140,247 (core)** | **39 %** | **91 %** | **0.84 / 0.88** |
| 140,150 to 140,700 (quiet zone) | 42 % | 89 % | 0.90 / 0.91 |
| 140,700 to 140,900 (outer flank) | 72 % | 88 % | 2.1 / 2.2 |
| 140,900 to 141,100 (outer edge) | 82 % | 90 % | 5.3 / 4.8 |

- A regular orbit has ⟨Y⟩ ≈ 2, so it would give λ = 4/t: 0.4 per year at 10 years and 0.13 per year at 30 years. The quiet zone stays at 0.9 per year. So it is chaotic, with a Lyapunov time of about 1.1 years (650 orbits), only less chaotic than its flanks.
- The class fractions depend strongly on the run length: 46.5 %, 66.3 % and 89 % chaotic at 5, 10 and 30 years. Of the particles that are chaotic at 5 years, 99.7 % are still chaotic at 10 years. "Bounded", "regular" and "uncertain" mostly mean "not chaotic yet". Read the map through λ, not through the classes.
- The longitude relative to Prometheus does not matter (scatter of the chaotic fraction over 36 longitude bins = counting noise).
- The "bounded" fraction has a comb in radius with a period of 8 to 10 km, probably the first-order resonances with Prometheus (spacing 7.7 km near m = 110). Not tested further.
- **Close encounters with Prometheus** (G1 open issue; 20,000 particles, 10 years, CPU, every step): 5.4 % come within 1 Hill radius (63 km), and 4.6 % pass inside the moon's radius. All particles that come within 3 R_H (12.8 %) start below 140,050 km, and all are chaotic. No particle that starts above 140,250 km comes within 7.3 R_H; the median closest distance at the core is 533 km (8.4 R_H). So the missing sub-stepping does not change the map from the core outward. Pandora was not checked (outer edge).
- A0 reran the 5-year map (2 × 10⁵ particles, GPU D+): the histogram and the radius profile are identical to the committed files.

### 2. The moons' share of the apse rate at the core (G0, question 4)

2,000 core particles (a = 140,222.43 km, e = 0.00235), 50 years, CPU f64. The rate is the slope of the apse longitude of the whole ring (fit of radius against longitude).

| Model | ϖ̇ (°/d) |
|---|---|
| Saturn J2 to J6 only | 2.698412 (PLAN 7.2: 2.698407) |
| + both moons (mean of 3 runs: base, other apse phase and seed, half step) | 2.6998 ± 0.0002 |
| Moons' share | **+0.0014 ± 0.0002** |
| + J8 to J12 (G0: +0.0001; the real planet has them) | 2.6999 |
| Observed | 2.70025 ± 0.00029 |

- The gap falls from 6 sigma (Saturn alone, G0) to 0.00035 °/d, which is 1.0 sigma with both errors. **The moons explain the G0 gap.**
- Caveat: the model's rate wobbles with the 6.2-year anti-alignment cycle of the moons. Over 10-year windows it goes from 2.6983 to 2.7008 °/d. This wobble is larger than the remaining gap, and the observation covers a few years of one phase. So the agreement cannot be tested better than about ±0.001 °/d without matching the phase of the cycle.
- The secular formula (Laplace coefficients) gives +0.00092 °/d, and it gets even the sign of Prometheus wrong. The core is at the m = 110 resonance with Prometheus, where the secular average fails. Single-moon runs do not split the share either, because without Pandora the orbit of Prometheus has no resonance and no 6.2-year cycle.
- A0 reran the no-moon and both-moon runs: 2.6984116 and 2.6997176 °/d, identical to A8's fits.

### 3. Lyapunov time of the moons (G1)

- **Correction to G1.** At G1, A0 read the growth of a moon offset between years 1 and 12 as a Lyapunov rate of 0.22 to 0.29 per year. Linear growth from Kepler shear alone gives a factor up to 12 in that time, which is ln(12)/11 = 0.23 per year. So the G1 rates were mostly shear, not chaos (`mistakes.md` M19).
- A first try with shadow orbits failed: the rate depended on the offset (0.95 per year at 1 cm, 0.09 at 100 m), because rounding noise grows by shear (M20). A8 used the tangent (variational) vector instead, integrated with IAS15 together with the orbit.
- Result, 100 runs of 100 years from the nominal state plus a 2 km position kick; rate = slope of ln(d) over years 30 to 100:

| Group | Runs | Rate (per year) | Lyapunov time |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaotic (slope > 0.1; gap in the slopes between 0.083 and 0.128) | 26 | 0.223 ± 0.061 (error of the mean 0.012) | 4.5 yr |
| not exponential (MEGNO rate 0.013) | 74 | 0.033 ± 0.010 | |
| nominal state, 8 tangent directions | 8 | 0.175 ± 0.015 | 5.7 yr |

- The chaos is intermittent: 9 of 100 runs change group between years 30 to 60 and 60 to 100.
- Literature (GR03): 0.30 to 0.35 per year. Our chaotic rate is 1.4 times lower. GR03 trace the chaos to the 121:118 resonance of the two moons, so the other moons are not a likely cause. The masses and eccentricities in `params/` set the resonance overlap. The difference is open.
- The real state of Prometheus is uncertain by about 10 km (G0), and one 2 km neighbourhood already holds both chaotic and regular runs. So a comparison with observations over decades needs several moon realisations (O7).
- A0 reran the first 20 of the 100 runs (84 s on 8 threads): identical, row for row.

## Decisions made in phase 2 (for AJ to accept or reverse)

1. **ADR 0006: precision of the GPU.**
   - Metal has no f64. Science runs use **D+**: position and velocity in double-single (two f32 per value, 48 B per particle), Kepler drift in double-single, the J2 kick in double-single, and J4, J6 and the moons as f32 magnitudes along the double-single position.
   - **C** (double-single throughout) is the GPU reference mode, at half the speed of D+.
   - **D** (all kick magnitudes in f32) is 17 % faster than D+ (1.11 × 10⁹ against 9.5 × 10⁸ particle-steps/s) but fails PLAN 10 in the tail. It stays as a fast mode that is not for science.
   - The web toy (W2) uses **A** (plain f32).
   - Metal fast math breaks plain double-single. The code has a guard, and a start-up self-test that fails closed.
   - Lesson (also in the ADR): 47 particles hid the tail of D. A precision claim needs the maximum and the 99th percentile over thousands of particles.
2. **ADR 0007: moons on the GPU from a host-computed kick table.** The CPU fills the moon positions and the indirect term in f64 for each block of steps, with the same function as the CPU backend, and uploads them. So the GPU and the CPU see the same moons, and there is no Hermite interpolation in WGSL. The web toy can do the same in wasm.
3. **ADR 0008 and ICR-003: chaos indicator.**
   - A shadow particle at d0 = 1 km (radial), renormalised every K = 100 steps (0.67 orbit), with MEGNO ⟨Y⟩ from the renormalised series.
   - Classes: regular ⟨Y⟩ ≤ 2.5, chaotic ⟨Y⟩ ≥ 5, uncertain between them. A1-ANALYSIS adds "bounded" (⟨Y⟩ < 1.5).
   - A plain sum of logarithms cannot classify, because Kepler shear makes a regular orbit grow by a factor of about 9,400 in 10³ orbits.
   - The chaos indicator halves the speed (2 times the work per step).
   - The done-when baseline displacement is 10⁻⁴ km (AJ approved on 2026-11-02). No tolerance was relaxed.
4. **Plots:** Python with Altair (Vega-Lite), in a separate uv project `crates/fring-analysis/python/`. Each figure is a PNG and a Vega-Lite JSON spec, which the web app can use later.

## Open issues and risks

- **Other GPUs.** The D+ result was not rerun with the WGSL worst-case model for division and square root (ADR 0006, risk 2). On a GPU other than the M4, run the start-up self-test and the G2 done-when test before a science run.
- **Close encounters.** Per-particle sub-stepping near Prometheus (PLAN 7.3) is not implemented. With d0 = 1 km the chaos indicator leaves its linear range in close encounters. A1-ANALYSIS reports the closest-approach distribution.
- **Indicator and run length.** ⟨Y⟩ grows with time for chaotic orbits, so only runs of equal length can be compared.
- **One bad particle stops a GPU run.** The first 30-year map over the full band stopped at "unbound or invalid orbit": Prometheus scattered one particle, and the whole run failed. For production runs with 10⁶ particles, a particle that becomes unbound or invalid should be flagged and frozen (or removed), not stop the run. A0 proposes an ICR for this in phase 4, with the sub-stepping work.
- **Speed without collisions.** All speeds above are for test particles.
- Albers et al. 2012 is still not verified (see G0).
