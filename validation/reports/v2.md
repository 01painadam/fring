# V2: Prometheus and Pandora over 20 years, REBOUND reference

Model (ADR 0004): Saturn is a free massive body at the origin, at rest at t = 0. Prometheus and Pandora start from `params/moons.toml` (Saturn-centred, Saturn-equator frame, epoch 2008-01-01 TDB). The J2, J4, J6 force of `params/saturn.toml` acts on the moons relative to Saturn's position. Saturn's reaction to the J force is neglected (relative order GM_moon/GM, about 3e-10). The moons attract each other and Saturn. IAS15, epsilon 1e-9, 20 Julian years (631152000 s), output every hour. Saturn's position and velocity are subtracted from the output, so every state is Saturn-centred. Units km, km/s, s.

## Chaos horizon

Second copies of the run with Prometheus displaced at t = 0 (difference of both moons, position, km):

| Offset | T(1 km) | |dr| at 20 yr (Prometheus / Pandora) |
|---|---|---|
| 1e-3 km along the velocity (pure time shift, no Kepler shear) | not reached in 20 yr | 0.19 / 0.19 km |
| 1e-6 km along the velocity | not reached | 5e-5 / 6e-5 km |
| 1e-3 km along x (nearly along-track, small radial part) | 2.50 yr | 3.1 / 2.7 km |
| 1e-3 km along y (nearly radial: changes a, so Kepler shear) | 0.087 yr (32 d) | 136 / 108 km |

T(1 km) depends strongly on the direction of the offset, because a radial offset changes the semi-major axis and the moon drifts in longitude linearly. The offset that isolates the chaos is the one along the velocity. The ratio |dr| / offset after 1, 3, 6, 12 and 20 yr is 17, 49, 81, 186, 269 for the 1e-3 km run and 12, 30, 57, 298, 82 for the 1e-6 km run. The two agree within a factor 3 (they would be identical for a purely linear response), so the divergence is mildly chaotic over 20 years and the response is of the order of 10 to 300 times the initial error.

Growth rate of ln |dr| (slope of the 10-day running maximum), per year, along-1e-3 run: 0.99 (0.2 to 2 yr, includes the initial transient), 0.21 (2 to 6), 0.15 (6 to 12), 0.00 (12 to 20, saturated), 0.14 over 0.2 to 20 yr; along-1e-6 run: 0.86, 0.24, 0.49, -0.13, 0.16. These are finite-time rates of a position difference, not a converged Lyapunov exponent (no MEGNO: REBOUND's variational equations do not include the custom J force). The literature value is 0.3 to 0.35 per year (GR03a, GR03b). Our 2 to 12 yr rates, 0.15 to 0.5, bracket it; the 20 yr mean of 0.14 to 0.16 is lower. A two-moon model without Saturn's other satellites need not reproduce a literature value, and 20 years is short.

Consequence for C3: compare the moon positions with the 6 h file within 1 km while the Rust integrator error times the response factor stays below 1 km. If the Rust run starts from the same state and has an integrator error of epsilon km, the difference after t years is roughly epsilon times 20 (t = 1 yr) to 300 (t = 12 yr). Report the first epoch at which Rust and REBOUND differ by 1 km; compare statistics (below) beyond it.

## Mean motions, resonance and closest approach

"Mean" elements here are orbit-averaged (one-period boxcar of the Saturn-centred two-body eccentricity vector and of the azimuth), not geometric elements. Mean motion is the central difference of the mean longitude over +-24.84 d (two synodic periods).

- Mean motion over 20 yr: Prometheus 587.28454 deg/day, Pandora 572.79030 deg/day (means). Peak-to-peak range of the 20-year series: Prometheus 2.27 deg/yr (std 0.51), Pandora 2.63 deg/yr (std 0.58). The literature zones of width 1.8 and 3.1 deg/yr (GR03b) are of the same order. Our ranges are close to each other; ours are the ranges seen in one 20-year orbit, so they may be smaller than a zone width from long runs. The two moons exchange angular momentum, so the two curves mirror each other (plot).
- Mean eccentricities: Prometheus 0.0022, Pandora 0.0042.
- Synodic period (time between conjunctions): 24.84 d (295 conjunctions), as in the literature (24.8 d).
- Apse anti-alignment: the difference of the mean longitudes of pericentre grows linearly; a linear fit gives an anti-alignment period of 6.24 yr (literature 6.2 yr), with anti-aligned apses at 5.11, 11.34 and 17.58 yr after the epoch.
- Closest approach: the radial separation at conjunction swings between 1547 and 3221 km. Its upper and lower envelopes are widest at the anti-alignment epochs (upper envelope peaks at 5.1, 11.3 and 17.6 yr). The lowest separations occur at 11.8 and 17.6 yr (about 1590 and 1550 km); the early part of the lower envelope is flatter (about 1900 to 2000 km at 2 to 8 yr), so the 6.2 yr cycle is clear in the spread and weaker in the minimum, which is also changed by the chaotic wander of the mean motions.
- Resonant angles phi_k = 121 lambda_Pd - 118 lambda_Pr - k varpi_Pr - (3-k) varpi_Pd, k = 0 to 3: none of the four librates throughout; each alternates between circulation and episodes of libration around turning points (about 4 to 7 yr, 10 to 12 yr and 17 to 19 yr), as expected for overlapping resonance components.

## Run times

Five runs in parallel (main plus four shadows) on 10 cores, a Python force callback: 790 to 910 s each. A single 20-year run takes about 13 to 15 minutes.
