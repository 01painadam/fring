# V1: single-particle precession, REBOUND reference

Setup: Saturn (GM, R, J2, J4, J6 from `params/saturn.toml`) at the origin, one massless particle, REBOUND 5.2.2 IAS15 (epsilon 1e-9), J force as an additional force written from PLAN 7.1. Start: guiding centre at r, z = 0, radial velocity e r kappa, tangential velocity r n, vertical velocity i r nu, e = i = 1e-3. 200 orbits, 100 samples per orbit. Units km, s, rad.

Measured rates are compared with PLAN 7.2: varpi_dot = n - kappa, node_dot = -(nu - n).

| r (km) | PLAN varpi_dot (rad/s) | event error | fit error | PLAN node_dot (rad/s) | event error | fit error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100000 | 1.828254e-6 | -7.40e-6 | -7.40e-6 | -1.811327e-6 | -6.46e-6 | -5.47e-6 |
| 140222.43 | 5.450937e-7 | -7.15e-6 | -7.20e-6 | -5.425779e-7 | -6.84e-6 | -5.98e-6 |
| 180000 | 2.250175e-7 | -7.03e-6 | -7.12e-6 | -2.243923e-7 | -4.17e-6 | -6.83e-6 |

All errors are below 1e-5 (0.001 %), 10 times better than the A6 target and 100 times better than the PLAN tolerance. The remainder is the finite-amplitude correction of order e^2 and i^2: with e = i = 1e-4 at the core radius the error falls to 2e-8 (apse) and 3e-7 (node). Changing the start phase or halving the sampling density changes it by less than 1e-9.

Conserved quantities over 200 orbits: Lz and the Jacobi energy are constant to 7e-16 and 2e-15.

Check of the reference force: gradient versus finite difference below 1e-8; REBOUNDx `gravitational_harmonics` (J2 and J4 only) gives the same trajectory as the reference force with J6 off, to 3e-10 km after 2 days (see `tests/test_force.py`).

Files: `reference/v1_precession.json` (all numbers and settings), `reference/v1_series_r*.csv` (states, 10 per orbit, columns t_s, x, y, z, vx, vy, vz in km and km/s), `reports/v1_precession.png`.
