# V3: REBOUND reference for test particles near the F ring core

**Read this first (bit-exact initial states).** The agreement target is 1 km after 100 orbits. A radial position offset of
only 1 m changes the semi-major axis by 2 m and grows to about 1.76 km by Kepler shear
over 100 orbits (median of 10 particles; estimate 1.5 x 2 m x 2 pi x 100 = 1.88 km). So a difference of about 1 m in a, or 1 part in 10^8 in
the mean motion, uses up the whole tolerance. The Rust test must read the initial states from `validation/reference/v3_ic.csv`
(17 significant digits, round-trip exact) and must not rebuild them from elements, and its moons must start from `params/moons.toml`
exactly as REBOUND's do.

Task V3 (PLAN section 9), serves C4. Role A6. All numbers below come from `validation/reference/v3.json`.

## Model
ADR 0004 as in V2: REBOUND 5.2.2, IAS15 with epsilon 1e-09, Saturn a free massive body, Prometheus and Pandora with
GM and states from `params/moons.toml` at t = 0 (the 2008-01-01 epoch), J2+J4+J6 acceleration from `fring_ref.force` acting on the moons and the
particles relative to Saturn, massless test particles, Saturn-centred outputs in Saturn's equator frame. The particles do not
act on the moons or on each other, and IAS15's step is set by the moons only (a particle run alone is bit-identical to the same particle
in the full run).

One orbit is T0 = 2 pi / n(a0) = 53445.914 s (14.846 h), with n(a0) the J2+J4+J6 circular-orbit
frequency at a0 = 140222.43 km (PLAN 7.2). 100 orbits are 61.86 days. Output every T0/10, 1001 times.

## Particles
Seed 20261101 (numpy PCG64). Per candidate, in this order: guiding radius a ~ U[a0 - 150, a0 + 150] km (a 300 km band),
epicyclic e ~ U[0, 0.001], epicyclic i ~ U[0, 0.0002] rad, then three phases (radial epicycle, vertical, longitude) ~ U[0, 2 pi).
Initial states are first-order epicycles about the circular orbit at a with the exact n, kappa, nu (`fring_ref.particles.epicycle_states`).
e and i are epicyclic amplitudes, not osculating elements (the J2 osculating e of a circular orbit is about 4.5e-3, see V1).

## Selection (non-chaotic orbits)
- Close approach: the minimum distance to either moon over the run (cubic Hermite between samples every T0/100) must be at least
  5 Hill radii (Hill radius 63.3 km Prometheus, 61.6 km Pandora).
- Shadow test: a second particle offset by 0.001 km along the velocity (no first-order change of a, so no Kepler shear) must stay within
  10 times the offset for the whole run. A quiet orbit gives a growth factor of about 3: a position
  offset with unchanged velocity excites an epicycle of the same size, which is not chaos. A larger factor means that a 1 m change grows,
  so differences between codes would grow too; this happens for passes at 6 to 10 Hill radii.
- Drawn 150, examined in draw order until 100 passed: 104. Rejected 4: 0 close approach, 4 shadow growth (0 both).
  Closest approaches of the rejected, in Hill radii: 9.36, 6.35, 9.76, 6.11. Passed but not needed: 0.
- Growth factor of the accepted: min 3.03, median 3.32, max 9.31
  (largest of all candidates 43.53). Over 100 orbits (shorter than the synodic period of about 111 orbits) true chaos is not
  expected; the rejections are sensitive encounters, not chaotic orbits.
- Closest approach among the accepted: 7.26 Hill radii.

## REBOUND's own error (floor of the reference)
Epsilon 1e-09 against 1e-11 for the same 100 particles: worst final position difference 9.85e-08 km, worst at any
output time 9.85e-08 km (per particle in `v3.json`, `error_floor`). Any Rust/REBOUND difference above this is not REBOUND's noise.
